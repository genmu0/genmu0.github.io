## 📖 Research Projects
I occasionally write blog posts about smaller snippets of research via $\LaTeX$.

My main research interests are long form research textbooks known as monographs.

1. **Boolean Statistical Mechanics**：My statistical mechanics monograph is available to compile from source [here](https://github.com/genmu0/BSM-Recolor). It is also available on Amazon in paperback and hardcover. I started on 2024-01-01 as a New Year's Resolution and completed on 2026-10-08. The work I am most proud of, but probably it has the least immediate influence to the world at large.
  - A solution to generalizing random walks to multiple particles is described as **Boolean random walks**. The appendix contains an algorithm to speed up the process via parallel computation. Boolean random walks are useful because they approximate simple fluid simulations, but they are also constrained by the computational complexity of cellular automata.
  - A theory of thermodynamics is developed for Boolean values on integer lattices. Since the theory is best described using the aforementioned Boolean random walks, hence the textbook title of **Boolean Statistical Mechanics**.
  - The appendix describes the low-rank approximation of stochastic matrices, titled **class stochastic matrices**. A formula to estimate the number of classes and an modified **k**-means clustering algorithm is described.
2. **Statistical Linguistics**：A statistics textbook on linguistics and language models. I am staunchly opposed to the use of statistical language models (SLMs) in order to get work done, and am disappointed at how "AI" corporations falsely advertise the capability of their products. My estimated start date is 2027-01-01.
  - A theory of concept spaces (nouns) and semantic operators (verbs) is formalized to describe how language *must* work in biological or artificial neural networks in order to function properly.
  - Classical linguistics and computational linguistics are compared in detail to tackle the differences between formal languages and informal languages (AKA natural language).
  - Fundamental problems and limitations of language and translation are discussed through the lens of computational statistics.
3. **Statistical Logistics**: A statistics textbook that intends to tackle logistics problems from the perspective of mathematics and statistics. It is my opinion that the applied mathematics field of logistics is underdeveloped, and future mathematicians will increasingly find their service to society as "logisticians".
  - Algorithmic approximations to **nurse scheduling problems** are described in order to automate schedules for businesses.
  - Transportation theory and optimal transport is discussed in relation to shipping and distribution of products.
  - The proximal optimization of an economy with **n**-tiers of production is discussed for directed acyclic graphs and directed cyclic graphs.
4. **Statistical Pedagogy and Epistemology**: A statistics textbook that tackles the problems of social education and the theory of knowledge. Education reformists are often concerned with improving public education, but the mathematical theory of learning is underdeveloped.
  - The theory of learning is discussed from the lens of entropy minimization, and the prioritization of learning is addressed with a cost function.
  - The theory of optimal attack describes the optimal path through a knowledge graph to maximize learning. It is named after how to seizing countries in *Risk* requires building up pieces; in our case, tackling topics requires building up prior knowledge.
  - The theory of knowledge, epistemology, is discussed in great detail under various assumptions of world models.

## Why do independent mathematics research?
The current state of academic publishing is relatively hostile to long-form math research. Only applied mathematics and physics that increases the wealth or secures the wealth of fat cats is given funding priority in academic institutions (read: energy and weapons, although weapons are merely the ability to \{store, direct, release\} energy). This is why our era has subtracted funding the humanities in secondary and post-secondary education to add funding to STEM. I do not oppose necessary militarization, but it has a cost.

I did some research during my time in graduate school, but I was unfortunately experienced academic dishonesty three times (yikes). I think it wise to not describe the academic institution in question.

While Terence Tao is a staunch believer of collaboration in mathematics research, I am less convinced. The current academic system prioritizes hiring by citation index, and the prestige (read: grant money) they bring to the institution. Unfortunately, systems like this are easy to game via Goodhart's law. The current method of gaming this system is mass publication (high quantity, low quality) and hyperauthorship (many authors, unequal contribution).

## Why not post on arXiv?
The professors from my graduate program do not post on arXiv and are therefore not endorsers. They don't need to secure tenure, so they don't need what arXiv does for someone's career.
